## Generated derivatives

The signal mark, product hero images, and family banner were generated with
OpenAI's built-in image generation system using the approved masters as
identity references. Derived icon and layout exports are deterministic local
builds from those approved raster sources.

The Crow Signal fonts and Crow Talon cursors are generated locally from
original source geometry in this repository. They consume no third-party font
or cursor binaries.
