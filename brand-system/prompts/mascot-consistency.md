# Crow Mascot Consistency Prompt

Use this prompt as a controlled template for new Crow mascot derivatives.
Attach exactly
`assets/mascots/masters/crow-mascot-v3.png` as the sole character identity
reference. Earlier head, humanoid, companion/pet, v1 and v2 files are inactive
legacy/reference records and must not be attached, blended, substituted or
reactivated.

## Fill before use

- **PRODUCT:** CrowClaw | Crow-GodMod3 | CrowQuant | CrowNest | CrowMemory | CrowFlix
- **PURPOSE:** hero | editorial scene | onboarding | avatar source | release art
- **SCENE:** describe the environment and action
- **COMPOSITION:** portrait | landscape | square, plus camera distance
- **CROP:** full crow | portrait | wing-and-head | avatar
- **ACCENT EMPHASIS:** choose approved cool accents for the product
- **OUTPUT SIZE:** exact pixel dimensions

## Prompt

Create a new **[PURPOSE]** image for **[PRODUCT]** using the approved
`crow-mascot-v3.png` master as the strict and sole character identity
reference.

The subject is Crow: the same naturally proportioned adult biomechanical
black-feathered crow shown in the master, with exactly three eyes—two physical
eyes in a matched pair and one centred forehead eye. Preserve the master
character's face, elongated tapered crow beak, adult-crow anatomy, feather
structure, proportions, silhouette and integrated cybernetic construction.
The head must read immediately as a real crow, and the full body must never
become humanoid.

Render the scene as **[SCENE]** in a **[COMPOSITION]** composition at
**[OUTPUT SIZE]**, using the **[CROP]** crop and **[ACCENT EMPHASIS]** accent
emphasis. Keep the subject readable at the intended size and reserve clean
negative space where product UI or separate typography will sit.

Use a nocturnal technical palette dominated by:

- void black and blue-black;
- graphite and gunmetal;
- deep navy and indigo;
- ultraviolet;
- electric blue and cyan.

Magenta may appear only as a restrained iris or pulse accent. Use cool steel
highlights on the beak and integrated mechanical anatomy. Lighting should be
precise, directional and dimensional, with local signal glow rather than a
full-image neon wash.

Apply the product binding:

- **CrowClaw:** local systems and permission geometry; cyan, ultraviolet and
  electric-blue emphasis.
- **Crow-GodMod3:** controlled recursion and data-space distortion;
  ultraviolet and cyan with a rare magenta pulse.
- **CrowQuant:** quantitative lattice and analytical signals; electric blue,
  cyan and cool success signal.
- **CrowNest:** linked nodes and network stewardship; bright indigo, cyan and
  violet.
- **CrowMemory:** protected archive and persistent recall; violet, cyan and
  electric blue.
- **CrowFlix:** cinematic framing, projection and signal flow; electric blue
  and violet with restrained magenta.

Product binding changes only crop, background and approved accent emphasis.
It must not alter the crow's anatomy, proportions, posture language,
cybernetics or identity.

Do not render any letters, words, logos, product names, signatures, watermarks
or UI text inside the image.

## Negative constraints

No missing eye. No fourth eye. No extra eye-like facial gems. No duplicated
face, beak, head, limb or wing. No moon head, orb head, owl face, plague mask,
human face or short hooked owl beak. No warm metallic focal point. No dominant
pink field. No baked-in typography. No humanoid body, human arms, hands,
clothing, costume, armour or upright human stance. No chick, toy, pet,
bobblehead or generic cartoon proportions. No decorative cybernetic jewellery.
No blur or glitch over the eyes. No crop through the forehead eye or beak tip.

## Final self-check

Before returning the image, verify:

1. Exactly three eyes are visible or anatomically unambiguous.
2. The subject reads as the same naturally proportioned adult crow shown in
   `crow-mascot-v3.png`.
3. The elongated crow beak and corvid silhouette remain intact.
4. Cybernetics read as integrated functional anatomy.
5. Cool black, blue, cyan, indigo and ultraviolet dominate.
6. Magenta is a small focal accent only.
7. The selected product binding is visible through crop, background and accent
   emphasis only.
8. No forbidden anatomy, alternate character, warm brand accent or generated
   text is present.
