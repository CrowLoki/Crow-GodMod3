# Crow Mascot Consistency Prompt

Use this prompt as a controlled template for new Crow mascot derivatives.
Attach exactly one approved master from `assets/mascots/masters/` as the
identity reference. Do not blend two forms into a new character.

## Fill before use

- **FORM:** Core Architect | Field Operator | Glitch Ascendant | Pet Companion
- **PRODUCT:** CrowClaw | Crow-GodMod3 | CrowQuant | CrowNest | CrowMemory | CrowFlix
- **PURPOSE:** hero | editorial scene | onboarding | avatar source | release art
- **SCENE:** describe the environment and action
- **COMPOSITION:** portrait | landscape | square, plus camera distance
- **OUTPUT SIZE:** exact pixel dimensions

## Prompt

Create a new **[PURPOSE]** image for **[PRODUCT]** using the approved
**[FORM]** master as the strict character identity reference.

The subject is Crow: a cybernetic black-feathered corvid with exactly three
eyes—two physical eyes in a matched pair and one centred forehead eye. Preserve
the master character's face, elongated tapered crow beak, feather structure,
proportions, silhouette and form-specific wardrobe or body plan. The head must
read immediately as a real crow.

Render the scene as **[SCENE]** in a **[COMPOSITION]** composition at
**[OUTPUT SIZE]**. Keep the subject readable at the intended size and reserve
clean negative space where product UI or separate typography will sit.

Use a nocturnal technical palette dominated by:

- void black and blue-black;
- graphite and gunmetal;
- deep navy and indigo;
- ultraviolet;
- electric blue and cyan.

Magenta may appear only as a restrained iris, gem or pulse accent. Use cool
steel highlights on the beak and mechanical parts. Lighting should be precise,
directional and dimensional, with local signal glow rather than a full-image
neon wash.

Apply the product binding:

- **CrowClaw:** practical local intelligence; Core Architect primary, Pet
  Companion compact; cyan and ultraviolet emphasis.
- **Crow-GodMod3:** controlled recursion and high power; Glitch Ascendant
  primary, Core Architect compact; ultraviolet and cyan with a rare magenta
  pulse.
- **CrowQuant:** analytical precision; Field Operator primary, Pet Companion
  compact; electric blue, cyan and cool success signal.
- **CrowNest:** network stewardship; Core Architect primary, Pet Companion
  compact; bright indigo, cyan and violet.
- **CrowMemory:** protected persistence; Core Architect primary, Pet Companion compact;
  violet, cyan and electric blue.
- **CrowFlix:** cinematic nocturnal energy; Field Operator primary, Pet
  Companion compact; electric blue and violet with restrained magenta.

Form-specific requirements:

- **Core Architect:** tall humanoid corvid, structured long coat, articulated
  talons, precise circuitry and controlled data geometry; composed authority.
- **Field Operator:** humanoid corvid in practical dark fieldwear, grounded
  ready stance, purposeful tool or local-system context; no generic mercenary
  styling.
- **Glitch Ascendant:** recognisable humanoid anatomy with expanded feather
  energy and controlled data-space distortion; face and eye pattern remain
  sharp and intact.
- **Pet Companion:** small non-humanoid natural crow body, expressive
  three-eye face, restrained cybernetic details and stable perch stance; no
  human torso or arms.

Do not render any letters, words, logos, product names, signatures, watermarks
or UI text inside the image.

## Negative constraints

No missing eye. No fourth eye. No extra eye-like facial gems. No duplicated
face, beak, head, limb or wing. No moon head, orb head, owl face, plague mask,
human face or short hooked owl beak. No warm metallic focal point. No dominant
pink field. No baked-in typography. No stock superhero armour. No medieval
fantasy costume. No generic mascot cartoon proportions. No blur or glitch over
the eyes. No crop through the forehead eye or beak tip.

## Final self-check

Before returning the image, verify:

1. Exactly three eyes are visible or anatomically unambiguous.
2. The subject reads as the same Crow character and the selected approved form.
3. The elongated crow beak and corvid silhouette remain intact.
4. Cool black, blue, cyan, indigo and ultraviolet dominate.
5. Magenta is a small focal accent only.
6. The selected product binding is visible without adding text.
7. No forbidden anatomy, warm brand accent or generated text is present.
