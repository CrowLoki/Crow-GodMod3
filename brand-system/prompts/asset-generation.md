# Artwork generation briefs

These are the reusable production briefs for extending the approved asset
family. Attach the appropriate approved master as the first identity reference.

## Signal mark

Create a square, front-facing head-and-collar emblem with exactly three eyes,
an elongated crow beak, layered black feathers, a three-segment broken halo,
and a strong small-size silhouette. Use black, gunmetal, indigo, ultraviolet,
electric blue, and cyan. Magenta is limited to the forehead-iris core. No text,
orange, gold, owl anatomy, moon head, or extra eyes.

## Product-neutral family banner

Place the Core Architect and Pet Companion in a wide, dark system space with
restrained data lattice and broken-halo geometry. Keep at least forty percent
quiet space for live product copy. Preserve each character's exact three-eye
identity. Do not bake text into the art.

## Product bindings

- CrowClaw: Core Architect; local action, permission, and system intelligence.
- Crow-GodMod3: Glitch Ascendant; controlled recursion and reality fracture.
- CrowQuant: Field Operator; quantitative lattice and analytical precision.
- CrowNest: Core Architect plus Pet; orchestration and linked nodes.
- CrowMemory: Core Architect; protected crystalline archive and recall.
- CrowFlix: Field Operator plus Pet; cinematic projection and signal flow.

Every product hero is wide and text-safe. It uses live HTML or native UI for
titles and controls. No product name, fake label, readable number, or logo is
baked into generated artwork.

## Global negative brief

No orange, amber, yellow, or gold dominance. No human face, owl face, plague
mask, moon/orb head, duplicated beak, extra eye, missing forehead eye, extra
limb, random weapon, generic superhero armour, drugs, dog, text, logo, or
watermark.
