# Artwork generation briefs

These are the reusable production briefs for extending the approved asset
family. Use
`assets/mascots/masters/crow-mascot-v3.png` as the sole active mascot identity
reference. All earlier head, humanoid, companion/pet, v1 and v2 files are
inactive legacy/reference records; do not use or blend them.

Mascot derivatives may change only crop, background and approved cool-accent
emphasis. Do not redraw the crow into another body plan, role, costume, age or
character.

## Signal mark

Create a square close crop from `crow-mascot-v3.png` with exactly three eyes,
the elongated crow beak and layered black feathers readable at small size.
Place a three-segment broken halo in the background without changing the
mascot. Use black, gunmetal, indigo, ultraviolet, electric blue and cyan.
Magenta is limited to a restrained iris or pulse. No text, orange, gold, owl
anatomy, moon head or extra eyes.

## Product-neutral banner

Place the single active adult biomechanical crow in a wide, dark system space
with restrained data lattice and broken-halo geometry. Keep at least forty
percent quiet space for live product copy. Preserve the master anatomy and
exact three-eye identity. Do not bake text into the art or add a second mascot.

## Product bindings

- CrowClaw: local action, permission and system intelligence; cyan,
  ultraviolet and electric-blue emphasis.
- Crow-GodMod3: controlled recursion and data-space distortion; ultraviolet,
  cyan and restrained-magenta emphasis.
- CrowQuant: quantitative lattice and analytical precision; electric-blue,
  cyan and cool-success emphasis.
- CrowNest: orchestration and linked nodes; bright-indigo, cyan and violet
  emphasis.
- CrowMemory: protected crystalline archive and recall; violet, cyan and
  electric-blue emphasis.
- CrowFlix: cinematic projection and signal flow; electric-blue, violet and
  restrained-magenta emphasis.

Every product hero is wide and text-safe. It uses live HTML or native UI for
titles and controls. No product name, fake label, readable number, or logo is
baked into generated artwork. Product identity comes from crop, background and
accent emphasis only; the mascot remains `crow-mascot-v3.png`.

## Global negative brief

No orange, amber, yellow, or gold dominance. No human face, owl face, plague
mask, moon/orb head, duplicated beak, extra eye, missing forehead eye, extra
limb, humanoid body, human arms or hands, clothing, costume, pet/chick/toy
proportions, alternate character, random weapon, generic superhero armour,
decorative cybernetic jewellery, drugs, dog, text, logo or watermark.
