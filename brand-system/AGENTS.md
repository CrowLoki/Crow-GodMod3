# Crow Brand System rules

This repository is the canonical, cross-project visual system for Crow products.

## Approved mascot identity

`assets/mascots/masters/crow-mascot-v3.png` is the sole active mascot for
CrowClaw, Crow-GodMod3, CrowQuant, CrowNest, CrowMemory, CrowFlix and future
Crow products. Product variants may change crop, background and accent emphasis
only. They must not introduce a new body plan, costume, role form or character.

Do not overwrite the approved master. New revisions must use a versioned sibling
filename until Crow explicitly approves replacing it.

Every earlier head, humanoid and companion file remains physically intact as an
inactive legacy/reference record. Never silently replace, delete, or reactivate
those files.

## Non-negotiable visual rules

- Every mascot has exactly three eyes: two physical eyes and one forehead eye.
- The head must read as a real elongated crow head with a tapered beak.
- The body is a naturally proportioned adult crow, never humanoid.
- Cybernetics are integrated through the feathers, face, torso, wings, legs and
  talons; they are anatomy, not jewellery or surface decoration.
- Never turn the head into a moon, orb, owl, plague mask, or human face.
- Core palette: black, gunmetal, navy, indigo, ultraviolet, electric blue, cyan.
- Magenta is a restrained iris/pulse accent.
- Orange, amber, yellow, and gold must never dominate the Crow Theme.
- Do not bake generated text, product names, or slogans into artwork.
- Product variants must preserve the same character anatomy and identity.
- Never turn it into a bobblehead, chick, toy, mascot suit, humanoid or
  human-clothed character.

## Asset discipline

- Files under `assets/mascots/masters/` and `assets/mascots/references/` are source
  material; derived assets belong in the other asset folders.
- Generated or converted assets must be reproducible through scripts where
  practical.
- Original font and cursor sources belong in `fonts/src/` and `cursors/src/`.
- Do not substitute third-party fonts or recoloured stock cursor packs for the
  original Crow Theme font and pointer families.
- Crow Signal v0.2 uses connected, hard-edged square-pixel strokes; do not
  revert it to a separated dot-matrix treatment.
- Crow Talon v0.2 uses crisp 32 px pixel-art source silhouettes with
  multi-resolution Windows exports; do not smooth them into generic neon
  pointers.
- Record source, prompt, generator, date, dimensions, and checksums in
  `provenance/`.
- Do not publish this repository or integrate it into another Crow project
  without Crow's explicit instruction.
