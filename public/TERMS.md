# Crow-GodMod3 research preview

Crow-GodMod3 is an independent, experimental research interface for working
with third-party or locally hosted language models. It is a rebranded
derivative of G0DM0D3 and is not an official G0DM0D3 hosted service.

## Responsible use

You are responsible for the prompts you submit, the model outputs you receive,
and anything you do with those outputs. Do not use this tool to violate
applicable law or to cause harm. The software is provided as-is, without
warranty.

## Data and providers

Conversations, settings, and configured keys are stored in this browser.
Requests are sent only to the model provider or local endpoint you configure.
This Crow-GodMod3 build does not publish application telemetry. A selected
provider and the site host may have their own logging and privacy practices.
