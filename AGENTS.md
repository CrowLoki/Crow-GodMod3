# Crow-GodMod3 project guidance

Crow-GodMod3 is an ongoing public project, not a disposable one-off clone.

- Preserve the exact product name `Crow-GodMod3`; the `3` is the final `E`.
- Phase 1 is the faithful, working standalone G0DM0D3 derivative with the
  CrowClaw visual identity.
- Phase 2 will add substantial new functionality. Do not pre-empt or invent
  Phase 2 features without an explicit request.
- Keep the standalone application independently deployable.
- Plan future CrowClaw support as an optional plugin, not a mandatory coupling
  between the two projects.
- Preserve upstream G0DM0D3 provenance, AGPL-3.0 licensing, and corresponding
  source availability.
- Preserve the CrowClaw dark black, magenta, violet, cyan, and teal identity.
  Do not introduce orange, amber, yellow, or gold visual styling.
- Treat `vendor/godmod3/index.html` as the unmodified upstream snapshot.
  Apply derivative changes through the maintained build layer and regenerate
  `public/crow-godmod3.html`.
- Keep telemetry disabled unless Crow explicitly approves a Crow-owned
  telemetry service and matching disclosure.
